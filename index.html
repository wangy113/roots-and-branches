How this lab works

About 10 minutes. Everything is a click. You make a prediction first, then the lab shows you what happened.

  1. Meet the family. Find out who is related to whom.
  2. Three moments. 632, 657, and 680. Each one leaves a question that never went away.
  3. Living branches. Match news from 2026 to the moment it echoes.
  4. Old roots or new reasons? Take one conflict apart.

Insight points. A right answer on the first try earns 2. A clue or a second try earns 1. Clues are there to help, so use them when you are stuck.
